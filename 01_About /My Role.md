# My Role

As a Mechanical Team Member in Team Anveshak, IIT Madras, I contributed to the design, development, and integration of mechanical subsystems for the Mars rover. My work involved taking components from the design stage through manufacturing support and assembly while ensuring they met functional and competition requirements.

## Key Responsibilities

* Designed mechanical components and assemblies using Fusion 360.
* Created CAD models, technical drawings, and design revisions based on team feedback.
* Participated in the mechanical design review process and improved designs through multiple iterations.
* Supported the manufacturing of designed components by preparing workshop-ready drawings and coordinating fabrication.
* Assisted in the assembly and integration of mechanical subsystems onto the rover.
* Worked with cross-functional teams to ensure compatibility between mechanical, electronics, and other rover subsystems.
* Contributed to the preparation and refinement of Rover Jade for competition.
* Participated in the team's campaign at the International Rover Challenge (IRC), where Team Anveshak secured **3rd Place**.
* Documented engineering decisions, design changes, and lessons learned throughout the development process.
