# Design Workflow

Every component followed a structured engineering workflow.

## 1. Understand the Problem

Identify the function of the component.

Questions considered:

- What should it do?
- What loads will it experience?
- Where will it be mounted?

---

## 2. Define Constraints

Examples

- Weight
- Space
- Manufacturing capability
- Material availability
- Ease of assembly

---

## 3. Concept Generation

Multiple concepts were discussed.

Advantages and disadvantages of each concept were evaluated.

---

## 4. CAD Modelling

Components were modelled in Fusion 360.

---

## 5. Design Review

Designs were presented during team reviews.

Feedback was incorporated.

---

## 6. Design Iteration

Models were modified until all requirements were satisfied.

---

## 7. Manufacturing Preparation

Engineering drawings were prepared.

Critical dimensions were verified.

---

## 8. Assembly

Components were integrated into the rover.

---

## 9. Testing

Components were evaluated during subsystem testing.

Design improvements were recorded.
